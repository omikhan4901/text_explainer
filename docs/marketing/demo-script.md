# Demo video script (90 seconds; record when the first release is out)

1. **(0–10 s)** A dense paragraph of a lease agreement open in a PDF reader.
   Voice: "This is the kind of text most of us skim and hope for the best."
2. **(10–25 s)** Select it, press Ctrl+Shift+Space. The card appears beside it and the
   plain version streams in. Point at "Grade 17 → 9".
3. **(25–40 s)** Click "Simpler": the same text, for a 12-year-old. Click "Clearer": same
   terms, untangled.
4. **(40–55 s)** In a browser, press Ctrl+C twice on a research abstract. Same card.
5. **(55–70 s)** Show the meaning check flagging a number (use a passage where the model
   drops one, if one turns up in testing; otherwise skip).
6. **(70–85 s)** Settings: the model catalog with "Fits easily", your own GGUF, the
   instructions editor.
7. **(85–90 s)** Turn Wi-Fi off and do it again. "Everything stays on your computer."
